Before you open your PR

  1. Click Download photo and save the file in the images/ folder, keeping the file name it gives you (for example images/your-name.jpg).
  2. In index.html, paste the copied card inside <div id="grid" class="grid">. The image src already points to your photo's file name.
  3. Open the site and check that search, your course filter, and View diary work. Take a screenshot and include it with your pull request.
